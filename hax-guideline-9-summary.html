Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 9 · "When wrong"

Support efficient correction

When the AI is only partly right, let people edit, refine, or recover instead of starting over.

The core idea

AI is sometimes only partially correct. To let people still reach their goal, make it easy to edit the AI's outputs.

Example from the page: a navigation app recommends a fast route, and the user can edit the route or part of it, for example to avoid an area they know is under construction.
