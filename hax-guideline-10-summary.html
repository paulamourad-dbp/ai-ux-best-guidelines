Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 10 · "When wrong"

Scope services when in doubt

When the AI isn't sure what you want, doing less (or asking) beats guessing.

The core idea

In ambiguous situations, less can be more. When uncertain about the user's goal, the AI should disambiguate (ask) or gracefully degrade its services. To do that, build it so it can compute its own uncertainty.

Example from the page: an assistant that calls people on demand isn't sure whom to call. Asking "Do you mean Bill G. or Bill C.?" costs less than calling the wrong person.

10CFall back to other strategies

When the main approach fails or is too uncertain, drop to a simpler one rather than failing outright. Slide the confidence down:

🤖 Full automation: the AI answers directly
❓ Ask a clarifying question: narrows the request
📋 Show a short list of options: user chooses
🧑 Hand off: link to a human or manual search
