Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 6 · "During interaction"

Mitigate social biases

Make sure your AI's language and behavior don't reinforce unfair stereotypes.

The core idea

Societal biases can sneak into AI systems through many routes. Treat this guideline as a reminder to plan for identifying, testing and mitigating fairness harms.

🎙️ Voice assistant: neutral by default

Avoid assuming gender or role from a request.

Assumes "Sure, honey! I'll add flowers to your shopping list."
Neutral "Sure. Added flowers to your shopping list."

📈 Workplace analytics: fair signals

Insights shouldn't penalise people for patterns that differ by group, such as working hours.

⚠️ Check: does this "engagement score" rank some groups lower for reasons unrelated to the work?

📱 Translation: avoid defaulting pronouns

When a source language has no gender, show options instead of guessing.

"They are a doctor."
Options: she is a doctor · he is a doctor · they are a doctor
