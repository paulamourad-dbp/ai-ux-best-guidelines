Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 11 · "When wrong"

Make clear why the system did what it did

Let people see an explanation of why the AI behaved the way it did, and use that power carefully.

The core idea

Make an explanation of the AI's actions or outputs available, as appropriate. It can be global (how the whole system works) or local (why this one output). Mix and match patterns, since explanation content and design strongly affect whether they help or distract.

Careful: explanations increase trust

⚠️ The page warns that the mere presence of an explanation has been shown to increase user trust. That can cause over-reliance and over-inflated expectations, so people may trust an AI even when it could be wrong (automation bias). For setting expectations, it points to Guidelines 1 and 2. It also mentions the InterpretML tool for improving model explainability.
