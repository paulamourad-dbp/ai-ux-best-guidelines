Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 13 · "Over time"

Learn from user behavior

Let the AI adapt to each person by learning from what they do, so it gets better over time.

The core idea

Use signals from the user to help the AI learn, improve, or personalize its services over time.

Example from the page: a writing assistant that learns your preferred style and adapts is better than one that keeps recommending content you've repeatedly dismissed or ignored.

The learning loop

👆 User acts (accept, dismiss, ignore)→ 📈 AI picks up the signal→ 🎯 Future suggestions fit better

My own simple framing of the guideline's idea. The page names accepting-style signals such as actions, dismissals and ignoring.

Other signals the AI could learn from

The page gives one example. These are my own suggestions for kinds of signals.

✅ Explicit actions

Accepting, editing, rating or choosing an option.

🙈 Dismissing or ignoring

Suggestions that are repeatedly skipped are a clue they don't fit.

🔁 Repeated patterns

Things you do the same way again and again.

🛒 Search and browsing

What you look for and click on, as in the shopping and search examples the page lists.
