Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 14 · "Over time"

Update and adapt cautiously

When the AI changes, keep the disruption small. Don't break what already works for people.

The core idea

Limit disruptive changes when updating or adapting an AI's behavior. Think about the scale and rate of change, and use user research to learn how changes may disrupt or impede people.

The model-update trap

⚠️ When updating a model, avoid introducing new errors for tasks it previously did well. The page suggests tools such as Backward Compatibility to check for this.

Illustration with made-up numbers: a new model improves on average but breaks something that used to work.

The two patterns

The page lists these two by title only. The explanations below are my interpretation of the names.

14AComprehensive updates

Bigger changes, delivered thoughtfully as one considered package, with notice and explanation.

🎉 What's new in v2
Smarter replies, a new layout, and a faster assistant.

Small improvements that appear right away and don't get in the way.
