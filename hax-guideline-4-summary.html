Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 4 · "During interaction"

Show contextually relevant information

Show what matters for this person, right now, in this place, doing this task.

The core idea

What people expect or need to see depends on their current task, location, or time of day. Capture those signals and use them to tailor what your AI shows.

Example from the page: a restaurant recommender should consider the user's location and show places nearby.

The recipe

📡 Capture context signals→🎯 Tailor the content→✅ User sees what's relevant

Signals can be

📍 Location🕐 Time of day📝 Current task👀 What's on screen

Do it in a privacy-aware way, weighing user benefit against privacy.
