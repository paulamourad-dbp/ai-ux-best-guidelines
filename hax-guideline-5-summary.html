Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 5 · "During interaction"

Match relevant social norms

Deliver your AI's behavior and presentation the way people expect, given their social and cultural context.

The core idea

An AI's services, behaviors and presentation should follow social and cultural norms. And norms vary across groups and cultures, so there's no single "right" style.

Example from the page: an informal tone can feel friendly in the United States and impolite in more formal cultures.

Try it: one message, different norms

The same assistant tells a user their order is delayed. Pick a context and see how the tone should shift. These are illustrative, since real norms vary a lot even within a country.

One-size-fits-all
Hey there! 👋 Bummer, your order's running late. Hang tight, we'll get it to ya ASAP!!

Same tone everywhere. Charming to some, jarring to others.

Norm-aware

What can "norms" cover?

🗣️

Tone and politeness

Informal vs formal, first-name vs titles, directness vs softening.

🎨

Presentation

Colors, icons, gestures and imagery can carry different meanings across cultures.

🤝

Behavior

When the AI speaks up, how it asks for things, how it handles personal topics.

👥

Who is represented

Whose names, faces, holidays and experiences show up as "normal".
