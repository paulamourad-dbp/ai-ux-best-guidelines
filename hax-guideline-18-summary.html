Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 18 · "Over time"

Notify users about changes

When the AI gets new or improved abilities, tell people so they can adjust what they expect from it.

The core idea

Inform the user when the AI adds or updates its capabilities. After major model updates, people may need to recalibrate their expectations, both of what the system can do (Guideline 1) and how well it does it (Guideline 2).

Example from the page: a fitness tracker that originally struggled with hills and stairs gets better at them. Telling users helps them make the best use of it.

The caution: updates can also make things worse

⚠️ The page notes that some model updates can degrade performance in some areas even if overall performance improves. Use tools like Backward Compatibility to check for new errors. And if a change does reduce quality somewhere, that's another reason to tell users.

What a good change notice includes

The page gives one example and no patterns. This is my own checklist for turning the idea into a message.

1. What changed
"Step counting on hills and stairs is more accurate."
2. How it affects you
"You may notice higher step counts on hilly walks."
3. What still doesn't work
"Cycling is still not supported."
4. Where to learn more
"See What's new."
🛠️ Update ships→📣 User is told→🎯 Expectations recalibrate
