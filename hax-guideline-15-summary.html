Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 15 · "Over time"

Encourage granular feedback

Give people easy ways to tell the AI what they like and what's wrong, right in the flow of using it.

The core idea

Let users give explicit feedback on outputs and behaviors during normal use, so they can steer how the AI evolves. Feedback also helps monitor that the system is working as intended.

Example from the page: an entertainment service that recommends content gives users controls to guide it toward what they're really interested in, for instance by encouraging them to rate each recommendation.
