Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 16 · "Over time"

Convey the consequences of user actions

Show people how what they do will change the AI's future behavior, so they can steer it with confidence.

The core idea

Immediately update or convey how user actions will impact the AI's future behavior. This helps people understand how their actions influence the system, so they interact with it more effectively later.

Example from the page: in a photo-tagging app, when a user starts tagging a particular person, show how that input will affect other photos, either before or after they act.

🔔 Still want this?

16DIn help and documentation

Explain the effects of actions where people look things up.

Help: Why does "Not interested" matter?
Choosing "Not interested" tells the system to show fewer similar items. It doesn't delete anything you've already saved.

Why this matters

🎓

People learn the rules

Seeing cause and effect teaches them how to steer the AI.

🛡️

Fewer surprises

Nobody is caught off guard when their feed or results change. My own summary of the idea.
