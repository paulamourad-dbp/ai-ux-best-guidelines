Microsoft HAX Toolkit · Guideline 17 · "Over time"

Provide global controls

Let people set, in one place, what the AI watches and how it behaves everywhere.

The core idea

Let users globally customize what the AI system monitors and how it behaves, with preferences that apply system-wide.

Examples from the page: turning on parental controls in a search engine (controlling behavior), or restricting a smartphone app from accessing location (controlling what information it collects).

Two kinds of global control

🎚️

How it behaves

AI settings or parameters, like parental controls or content filters.

👁️

What it monitors or collects

Data access, like location, activity history or contacts.

These two groups come directly from the page's two examples. The names are my own shorthand.

Why "global"?

Scattered per-screen settings
Search: location
Maps: location
Photos: location

Easy to miss one. Hard to trust.

One global setting
Location access
Applies to Search, Maps and Photos.

One decision, applied everywhere. My own illustration of the "system-wide" idea.

Design tips (my suggestions)

The page itself is short. These are my own practical notes, not text from the page.

🔎 Make them findable

A clearly named Settings area, linked from where the AI acts.

🗣️ Use plain language

Say what a switch does in everyday words, and what changes when it's off.

↩️ Make them reversible

Easy to turn back on or reset to defaults.

🔗 Pair with other guidelines

Guidelines 3 and 4 (context-based timing and information) both suggest pairing with global controls.
